Thank you for the detailed report — the iPad Air 11-inch (M3) detail was
what let us find this.

WHAT WAS WRONG

The app is iPhone-only (UIDeviceFamily 1), so on iPad it runs at roughly
375 points wide. Several layouts overflowed at that width and wrapped
text mid-word, which is what made them hard to read:

- The bottom navigation bar needed more width than was available, so the
  "capture" button label broke across two lines as "capt / ure".
- On the daily check-in, the summary line, the hint text and the button
  were on a single row and collided.
- On the schedule, the time column was 38 points wide, so times such as
  "10:30a" split onto two lines.

This was not specific to iPad. The same layouts break on an iPhone SE,
which is how we reproduced and fixed it.

WHAT CHANGED IN BUILD 8

- Button labels are now limited to a single line, so no label can break
  mid-word anywhere in the app.
- The navigation bar spacing was reduced so all three buttons fit at 375
  points with their labels intact.
- The check-in row now stacks the summary and hint vertically instead of
  placing them side by side.
- The schedule time column was widened and is now limited to one line.
- Content width is capped so the layout stays legible if the window is
  resized wider on iPad.

HOW IT WAS VERIFIED

Reproduced and confirmed fixed on:
- iPad Air 11-inch (M3), iPadOS 26.5 — the device in your report
- iPhone SE (3rd generation), iOS 26.5 — smallest supported width
- iPhone 16 Pro, iOS 26.6 — physical device

No functional changes. The user flows are identical to the screen
recording provided previously.
