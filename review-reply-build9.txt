FIXED IN BUILD 9

The screenshot you attached is our first-run setup screen, and it had two
distinct faults:

1. The time labels ("8:00a", "10:00p") were held in fixed-width boxes. When
   the row ran out of horizontal space the box was compressed and the text
   wrapped inside it, so a time rendered as "10:0" above "0p".

2. The list of times was in a fixed, non-scrolling container. When the rows
   grew taller than the screen they overflowed instead of scrolling, and the
   "that's my day" button was drawn underneath them.

ROOT CAUSE

This was not specific to iPad. The app is built for iPhone
(UIDeviceFamily = 1), and on iPad it renders at roughly 375 points wide. Every
one of these faults reproduces identically on an iPhone SE, which is how we
found the rest of them.

We audited the whole app for the two underlying patterns rather than fixing
only the screen in the screenshot:

- Fixed-width text labels that could be compressed until the text wrapped
  inside the box. Found and fixed in four places: first-run setup, edit day,
  the daily check-in, and the workout set counter. Each now has a minimum
  width, cannot shrink, and is limited to a single line.

- Screens with no scroll view, which overflow instead of scrolling on a short
  screen. Found and fixed on four screens: setup, check-in, low-capacity mode,
  and the focus timer. All of them scroll now while keeping their layout.

We also stopped button labels from ever breaking mid-word, which was causing
"capture" to render as "capt" / "ure" in the bottom bar.

VERIFIED ON

iPhone 16 Pro, iOS 26.6 — physical device
iPhone SE (3rd generation), iOS 26.5 — narrow width, where these reproduce
iPad Air 11-inch (M3), iPadOS 26.5 — the configuration you reported

NO FUNCTIONAL CHANGES

Build 9 contains layout fixes only. No features, screens, permissions or flows
were added or removed, so the screen recording already in this thread still
reflects the app accurately.
