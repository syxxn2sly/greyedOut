Greyed Out — Privacy Policy

Last updated August 18, 2026

Greyed Out does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

No accounts, no servers

Greyed Out has no sign-up, no login, and no backend server. There is nothing to send your data to — the app makes no network requests at all, aside from downloading its own update files through the App Store.

What's stored, and where

Everything you enter — tasks, your schedule, anchor times, meals and go-to foods, workout templates and logs, and your preferences — is saved only in your device's local storage. None of it is copied anywhere else, by us or by anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Calendar access

If you choose to import events from your calendar, Greyed Out asks iOS for permission to read your calendar. This access is:

  • Read-only. The app never creates, edits, or deletes anything in your calendar.
  • Local-only. Events you import are copied into the app's own local storage as schedule blocks. They are never transmitted anywhere.
  • Optional. The app works fully without it. Denying or later revoking the permission (Settings → Greyed Out → Calendars) only disables the import feature.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

Greyed Out does not use analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising networks, or any other third-party tracking tools. Nothing about your usage is measured or reported.

Children's privacy

Greyed Out is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does transmit data — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.
