how it thinks

the rules the whole app follows

01

it notices, it doesn't nag. the line at the top of the day states a fact — food's handled, water's at zero — and stops. what you do about it is yours.

02

effort counts. a ten-minute version of the workout is logged as done, not as a lesser thing. bailing on a focus block still counts, because you started.

03

reminders stop asking. water and food nudges escalate three times over three hours, then go quiet. an app that nags forever gets uninstalled or ignored — both cost you the thing it was for.

04

nothing is typed that could be tapped. weight and reps are numbers with a unit toggle, not a sentence you have to type out by hand.

⌁

no accounts, no ads, no analytics. nothing leaves your device except calendar events you explicitly choose to import — copied in, never sent anywhere, never written back. read the full privacy policy.
